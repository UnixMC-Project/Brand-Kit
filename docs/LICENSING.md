## 2. UnixMC artwork – CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

Full legal text: [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE) · human summary: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/>

- **BY – Attribution:** you must credit UnixMC, link the licence and indicate that you did not change the file. A ready-made credit line is in the [Usage Policy](USAGE_POLICY.md#5-how-to-give-attribution).
- **NC – NonCommercial:** no use primarily intended for commercial advantage or monetary compensation.
- **ND – NoDerivatives:** if you remix, transform or build upon the material you may not distribute the modified result. UnixMC treats proportional scaling, format conversion and plain placement as technical handling, not as a derivative (see [Usage Policy §2](USAGE_POLICY.md#2-always-allowed-no-need-to-ask)).
- The licence does **not** cover trademarks or publicity/privacy rights (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 §2(b)); the Usage Policy does.
- Anything beyond this licence needs **written permission** from UnixMC.

> **History note (kit v2.0.0):** before this release the repository's `LICENSE` file contained *CC0 1.0 Universal* (public-domain dedication), which contradicted the README's CC BY-NC-ND statement and the copyright notice. The licence was aligned to **CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, the one the README has always declared. Copies of the repository downloaded **before** this change were obtained under whatever licence applied at the time; the old text remains visible in the git history.

What the OFL means in practice:

- ✅ You may use the fonts freely, including commercially, embed them in websites/apps/videos, and use them in documents. The licence does **not** pass onto documents or artwork you create with them.
- ✅ You may redistribute them with the licence text and copyright notice (both are in `licenses/third-party/`; include them if you pass the fonts on).
- ❌ You may **not sell the font files by themselves**.
- ❌ A *modified* font must not use the Reserved Font Name and must stay under the OFL.
- The font licence applies **only** to the font files. The UnixMC artwork that happens to be *set* in these fonts is still CC BY-NC-ND.
