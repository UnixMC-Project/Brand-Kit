# UnixMC Brand Usage Policy

*Applies to the UnixMC name, logos, titles, avatars, banners and all other files in this repository.*

This policy explains in plain language what you can and cannot do. The legally binding licence is [Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0](../LICENSE); this policy describes how UnixMC applies it and adds brand-protection rules. If anything here conflicts with the licence text, the licence text controls for copyright, and this policy controls for use of the UnixMC **name and identity**.

## 1. Who owns what

- The logos, titles, avatars, banners, badges and other artwork are © **UnixMC**.
- "UnixMC", the Clock mark and the title wordmark identify the UnixMC server and community. The licence grants **copyright** permissions only; **no trademark, endorsement or affiliation rights are granted** by it.
- Fonts are third-party and keep their own licence (see [Licensing](LICENSING.md#fonts)).
- Minecraft is owned by Mojang / Microsoft. UnixMC is independent (see [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md)).

## 2. Always allowed (no need to ask)

Provided you give attribution (see §5) and use files **unmodified** from this kit:

| Use | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Editorial & informational** | News articles, reviews, wiki pages, blog posts, guides about UnixMC |
| **Server listings** | Minecraft server lists and directories that list `play.unixmc.com` |
| **Community links** | Linking UnixMC from your own site, Discord, forum signature, README, video description |
| **Non-commercial fan content** | Videos, streams, screenshots, art and posts that *refer to* UnixMC. The logo may appear as an unmodified identifier of the server |
| **Technical adaptation** | Proportional scaling (nearest-neighbour for pixel art), format conversion (e.g. PNG → WebP), placing on a plain or photo background, cropping to a platform's circle/safe area **without** altering the artwork |
| **Reference & archiving** | Personal archiving, research, teaching, news reporting |

> "Proportional scaling, format conversion and placement" are treated as *technical* handling, not as creating a derivative work.

## 3. Needs written permission first

Ask before you:

- Use the name or logo **commercially** in any way: merchandise, sponsored content, ads, paid products, stores, monetised services, NFTs, paid events or subscriptions.
- Enter a **partnership, sponsorship or collaboration** that shows the UnixMC mark next to another brand.
- **Modify** the artwork: recolour, redraw, animate, remix, add text/effects, make new role/season variants, or build a derivative from the Blockbench model.
- Use "UnixMC" or the mark in a **name, domain, handle, bot, server, plugin, modpack or resource pack title**.
- Use the mark on a **different server or community** to represent it.
- Use the artwork in **physical** products or print runs.
- Use the artwork in a way that suggests UnixMC **endorses** you, your product or your opinions.

How to ask: see [Contact](#7-how-to-request-permission). Permission is only valid when it is **in writing**, from the UnixMC team, and for a **stated purpose**.

## 4. Never allowed

- Presenting UnixMC assets as your own, or removing the copyright/attribution requirement.
- Using the brand in any way that is **false, misleading, defamatory, hateful, sexual, illegal or harassing**, or that damages the reputation of UnixMC or its community.
- Impersonating UnixMC staff, the server, or official accounts; creating look-alike accounts, sites, stores or servers.
- Using UnixMC marks in connection with **scams, phishing, cheats, exploits, or unauthorised paid services** (including selling in-game ranks or items "from UnixMC").
- Using any asset in a way that implies it is **Minecraft, Mojang or Microsoft** content, or an official Minecraft product.
- Sublicensing, reselling or bundling the assets in any pack/library that you sell or that is behind a paywall.

## 5. How to give attribution

Attribution is required by the licence. Place it near the asset, or in the credits/description/footer if space is limited:

> **UnixMC logo © UnixMC – licensed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-nd/4.0/)**

Short form for tight spaces: `Logo © UnixMC (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)`

Good attribution also states **where** you got it (link to this repository) and that you **did not change** it.

## 6. Minecraft and Mojang

- UnixMC is **not an official Minecraft product** and is **not approved by or associated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft**.
- If you write about UnixMC, you may say *"a Minecraft Java + Bedrock server"* to describe what it is.
- Do **not** combine the UnixMC mark with the Minecraft logo, or use the Minecraft logo as part of anything built from this kit.
- Many banners include in-game Minecraft imagery. The licence in this repository applies to UnixMC's own artwork and compositions; anything owned by Mojang/Microsoft stays under their terms (see the [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)).

## 7. How to request permission

1. Open an issue at [github.com/UnixMC-Project/Brand-Kit/issues](https://github.com/UnixMC-Project/Brand-Kit/issues) titled **"Permission request – <your project>"**, or contact the UnixMC team in-game at `play.unixmc.com`.
2. Include: who you are, the project, **exactly** which assets you want, where and for how long they'll appear, whether money is involved, and a mock-up if it involves modification.
3. Wait for a written reply. Silence is **not** permission.

## 8. Reporting misuse

If you see the brand used against this policy (fake servers, scams, stolen artwork), report it through the same channel with a link or screenshot. Please do not harass the other party; let the team handle it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

UnixMC may update this policy and the kit at any time. Updates are listed in the [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md). Permissions already granted in writing stay valid for their stated purpose and term.
